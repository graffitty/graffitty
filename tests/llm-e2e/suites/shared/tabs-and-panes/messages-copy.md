Open Graffitty messages containing Unicode text. Click the message body and press Cmd+C on macOS or Ctrl+C elsewhere: the clipboard must contain all messages. Double-click a word and copy again: only the selection must be copied. Dismiss the panel and verify the shortcut no longer copies diagnostics. The panel is a dock panel: by default it takes its own row in the bottom band between the terminal and the status line, between the left and right docks, and the terminal area shrinks while it is open. Open log and Dismiss sit beside the text; copying belongs to text selection and the platform shortcut. Run in a rendered desktop with a real clipboard and restore its previous contents afterward.

On iPad, open enough text to scroll in Messages, Open log, and About. With a finger, pull the text past the top and bottom. The text should move under the pull while the scrollbar track stays against the viewport edge and the thumb shortens against the pulled end. Release and verify the text springs back and the normal scrollbar returns.

Hold a word in each iPad viewer, extend to an adjacent word, lift and tap Copy. Paste into a separate field and verify the selected Unicode text. Drag immediately on the same text and verify it scrolls.
